🎂 Cake Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The short version

Cake keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing you add to Cake is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What Cake stores

The names, dates, notes, reminder settings, and contact links you add are saved on your device. A small copy of upcoming dates is shared with Cake's own widget, also on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include this data, as it does for other apps.

Contacts

If you allow access, Cake uses your contacts to:

  • import birthdays you already have,
  • show a linked person's photo and name,
  • find their phone number when you choose to text them, and
  • save a birthday or anniversary to a contact's card when you link them.

Contact information is read and written on your device only and is never uploaded. You can turn off access anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Contacts.

Notifications and messages

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. When you send a message, Cake opens Apple's Messages composer with your text filled in; Cake never sends messages itself and can't see what you send.

Sharing

When you choose to share a birthday, the link contains only the name, date, and type. Your personal notes are never included.

Children

Cake is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete any person in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Dates saved to your contact cards stay in Contacts until you remove them there.
