I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are on in Settings › Notifications › Cake. You can change the reminder time and how far ahead you're reminded in Cake › ··· › Settings. iOS limits how many reminders an app can line up, so opening Cake every so often keeps your upcoming ones scheduled.

Importing birthdays

Tap ··· › Import Birthdays. People who already have a birthday saved can be added in one go; for everyone else, tap Add Date and choose it.

If you skipped Contacts at first, Import from Contacts will ask again. If Contacts access is off, it shows a button to turn it on in Settings, and if you shared only some contacts, tap Choose More Contacts.

Not many birthdays in Contacts?

Most people don't save birthdays in Contacts. In Cake › ··· › Import Birthdays you can also:

Ask friends for their birthdays. Send them a link; they add their birthday and text it back, and tapping their reply adds them.

Find birthdays in your calendars, like Google's Birthdays calendar or yearly events such as “Mom's Birthday”.

Use birthdays from Gmail. Turn on Contacts for your Google account in iPhone Settings › Apps › Contacts › Contacts Accounts, then import again.

Linking a contact

Linking someone to their contact shows their photo, lets you text them from Cake, and saves the birthday or anniversary to their contact card. To unlink, edit the person and open More Options.

Showing someone's age

The birth year is optional. When adding or editing someone, tap Add Year to show how old they're turning.

Widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add the Cake widget to see who's next.

Deleting someone

Swipe left on their row, or open them and tap Delete at the bottom. Dates saved to a contact card stay in Contacts until you remove them there.
